Common Questions

Enrolment FAQs

Answers to the questions we hear most before enrolment.

Students can typically start within 1–2 weeks of confirmation. We work with your timetable to find the best session slot. Mid-term enrolments are welcome.
All fees are managed by Vinsara Institute of Education. After your enrolment is confirmed, they will send you a full fee schedule and invoice. Payment plans are available on request.
Yes — many of our VCE students attend both Biology and Chemistry sessions. Just select one program in the form and mention the others in the notes field. We'll plan the timetable around both.
Our online sessions use interactive whiteboards, screen sharing, and real-time feedback tools. Many students find them just as engaging and productive as face-to-face sessions.
We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations. In most cases we can arrange a makeup session or provide notes and materials for the missed class so your student doesn't fall behind.
