About this website

This website is operated for SciMind Education, based in Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. For privacy-related enquiries, contact hello@scimind.com.au.

What you may choose to share

If you contact us by email, you may provide your name, email address, student's year level, subject of interest and details about your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or medical information in an initial enquiry.

How enquiry information is used

Information sent to SciMind may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss educational services and communicate about requested arrangements. We do not include a website contact form in this initial release.

Third-party services and links

This website may link to external services, and uses externally hosted fonts. External providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. Review the privacy practices of those providers when following their links.

Requests and corrections

You may contact SciMind Education to request access to, correction of, or information about personal details you have provided, subject to applicable law.
