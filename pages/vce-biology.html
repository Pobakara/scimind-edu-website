About This Program

Built for VCE Biology Success

SciMind's VCE Biology program provides the structured, expert teaching that students need to thrive in one of VCE's most demanding science subjects. Each session connects core biological concepts — from cellular mechanisms to evolutionary theory — through a lens of genuine scientific understanding rather than rote learning.

We align every session to VCAA's unit outcomes, with targeted SAC preparation, past exam question analysis, and ongoing diagnostic feedback so students always know where they stand.

  • Full Units 1, 2, 3 & 4 coverage
  • SAC preparation for every assessment
  • Exam strategy and extended response technique
  • VCAA exam-style question practice each session

Curriculum

What We Cover

Full Units 1–4 curriculum, VCAA-aligned. Click any unit to expand the topic list.

  • • Cell theory & prokaryote/eukaryote comparison
  • • Cell organelles: structure and function
  • • Cell membrane — fluid mosaic model
  • • Transport: diffusion, osmosis, active transport
  • • Photosynthesis — light & dark reactions
  • • Cellular respiration — aerobic & anaerobic
  • • Ecosystem structure & biotic/abiotic factors
  • • Energy flow & nutrient cycling
  • • DNA structure — double helix & base pairing
  • • DNA replication & protein synthesis
  • • Mitosis & meiosis
  • • Mendel's laws of inheritance
  • • Monohybrid & dihybrid crosses
  • • Sex-linked inheritance & pedigree analysis
  • • Mutations — types and implications
  • • Biotechnology: PCR, gel electrophoresis, CRISPR
  • • Enzymes & biochemical pathways
  • • ATP production — glycolysis, Krebs, ETC
  • • Photosynthesis — Calvin cycle
  • • Cell signalling — receptors & transduction
  • • Innate immune response
  • • Adaptive immunity — B & T lymphocytes
  • • Antigens, antibodies & vaccination
  • • SAC 3 preparation & extended response technique
  • • Darwin's theory of natural selection
  • • Genetic variation — sources & importance
  • • Evidence for evolution — fossil & molecular
  • • Speciation — allopatric & sympatric
  • • Human evolution — hominid differences
  • • Epigenetics & gene regulation
  • • VCAA past exam walkthrough
  • • Extended response writing technique
