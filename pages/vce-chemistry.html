About This Program

Built for VCE Chemistry Success

SciMind's VCE Chemistry program bridges the gap between textbook understanding and exam-ready performance. Chemistry is a subject that demands both conceptual clarity and precise mathematical skill — our program develops both simultaneously through structured problem-solving and deep content teaching.

Sessions are aligned to VCAA outcomes and regularly include exam-style questions, SAC simulations, and worked calculation practice so students develop genuine fluency rather than surface familiarity.

  • Full Units 1, 2, 3 & 4 coverage
  • Calculation fluency & mole concept mastery
  • SAC preparation for every assessment type
  • VCAA-style question practice each session

What We Cover

Full Units 1–4 curriculum, VCAA-aligned. Click any unit to expand topics.

  • • Atomic structure & electron configuration
  • • Periodic table trends
  • • Ionic, covalent & metallic bonding
  • • Molecular polarity & intermolecular forces
  • • Properties of metals, ceramics & polymers
  • • Alloys & composite materials
  • • Introduction to mole concept
  • • Empirical & molecular formulae
  • • Properties of water & hydrogen bonding
  • • Solubility & concentration calculations
  • • Acid-base reactions — Brønsted-Lowry theory
  • • pH, titration & neutralisation
  • • Oxidation & reduction — half-equations
  • • Redox calculations & stoichiometry
  • • Precipitation reactions
  • • Gravimetric & volumetric analysis
  • • Chemical equilibrium — Le Chatelier's principle
  • • Equilibrium constant expressions (Keq)
  • • Acid-base equilibria & Ka/Kb
  • • Buffer solutions
  • • Galvanic & electrolytic cells
  • • Standard electrode potentials
  • • Faraday's laws of electrolysis
  • • Industrial applications — Haber, Contact processes
  • • Organic functional groups & naming
  • • Reactions of organic compounds
  • • Polymers — addition & condensation
  • • Biochemistry — lipids, proteins & carbohydrates
  • • Spectroscopic analysis — IR, MS, NMR
  • • Chromatography techniques
  • • VCAA past exam analysis & walkthroughs
  • • Extended response technique
