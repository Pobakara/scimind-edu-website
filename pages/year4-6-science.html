About This Program

Science Should Spark Wonder

Primary school is the best time to build a genuine love of science. At SciMind, our Year 4–6 program is designed around curiosity — we ask big questions, explore ideas hands-on, and help students develop the scientific thinking skills that underpin all future science learning.

Every session covers real scientific concepts from the Australian Curriculum in a way that feels exploratory and rewarding. We build vocabulary, inquiry habits and problem-solving skills — the foundation for success in secondary science and beyond.

  • All four Australian Curriculum primary science strands
  • Curiosity-first, inquiry-based learning approach
  • Scientific vocabulary and communication skills
  • Confident Year 7 science readiness

Curriculum

What We Explore

All four Australian Curriculum primary science strands, explored through questions, activities and real-world connections.

  • • Needs of living things — food, water, air, shelter
  • • Plant structures & how they work
  • • Animal adaptations & survival strategies
  • • Food chains & food webs
  • • Ecosystems — habitats & biodiversity
  • • Human body systems overview (Yr 5–6)
  • • Properties of solids, liquids and gases
  • • Physical vs chemical changes
  • • Dissolving, mixing and separating materials
  • • Reversible and irreversible changes
  • • Everyday materials and their properties
  • • Introduction to atoms and elements (Yr 6)
  • • Forces — push, pull, gravity, friction
  • • Light — reflection, refraction, colour
  • • Sound — vibration, pitch & volume
  • • Electricity — circuits & conductors (Yr 6)
  • • Energy types — heat, light, movement
  • • Machines & simple mechanisms
  • • Earth's surface — rocks, soil & landforms
  • • Seasons & why they happen
  • • Weather patterns & the water cycle
  • • The solar system — planets & movements
  • • Day, night & phases of the Moon
  • • Environment & sustainability (Yr 5–6)
Who Is This For?
