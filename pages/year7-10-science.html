About This Program

Science That Sets Students Up for VCE

The Year 7–10 window is critical. The science habits, vocabulary, and reasoning skills developed during these years directly shape how well students cope with VCE. SciMind's Year 7–10 program ensures students build genuine understanding — not just enough to pass tests, but enough to excel when the pressure increases.

We cover all four Australian Curriculum science strands with a strong emphasis on scientific inquiry, data skills, and conceptual thinking. Each year level builds progressively on the last.

  • All four Australian Curriculum science strands
  • School assessment and test preparation
  • Scientific method, inquiry and data analysis skills
  • Progressive VCE readiness from Year 7 onwards

Curriculum

What We Cover

All four Australian Curriculum science strands, taught across Years 7–10. Click a strand to expand topics.

  • • Cells — structure, function & processes
  • • Body systems — digestive, circulatory, nervous
  • • Classification of living things
  • • Genetics, heredity & variation
  • • Ecosystems, food webs & energy flow
  • • Evolution & natural selection (Yr 10)
  • • States of matter & particle theory
  • • Atoms, elements & the periodic table
  • • Chemical vs physical changes
  • • Compounds & mixtures
  • • Acids & bases — key reactions
  • • Chemical equations & balancing (Yr 9–10)
  • • Forces — gravity, friction, applied forces
  • • Motion — speed, velocity & acceleration
  • • Energy — types, transfer & conservation
  • • Electricity & circuits
  • • Waves — sound & light
  • • Newton's laws of motion (Yr 9–10)
  • • Solar system & space exploration
  • • Earth's geological processes & plate tectonics
  • • Rock cycle & rock types
  • • Weather patterns & climate systems
  • • Earth's resources & sustainability
  • • Stars, galaxies & the universe (Yr 10)
Who Is This For?

Is This Program Right for You?

  • Year 7–8 students starting secondary school who want to build strong individual science habits from the start
  • Year 9–10 students who want to improve school grades and prepare for VCE subject selection
  • Students who find science interesting but need support with concept clarity and assessments
  • High-achieving students who want to extend their understanding beyond school content
  • Year 10 students planning to study VCE Biology or Chemistry who want a smooth transition
